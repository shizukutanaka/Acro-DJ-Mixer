Gestures — press ? or Esc to close

click / tap — latching toggles everywhere

hold ≥250 ms — momentary: EQ kills, Mut, FX, sampler pads, Mic, Phones, Slip, Mono

double-click — reset a knob/fader/readout to centre

wheel — fine-trim any knob or fader; on a loaded pad it trims that pad's level

shift — the alternate: clear hot cue, free-size loop in/out marks, phase-only sync, bar jump, half-beat roll, jog scrub, loop-edge trim, save loop memory, solo phones, resample an armed loop to an empty pad, clear a pad

alt — the expert layer: sync leader, stop a sampler voice, second loop-memory slot, trim the loop out-point

right-click — cue-bus preview on hot-cue and sampler pads

library — type to filter · ▶ previews on the cue bus · × deletes (two-click confirm) · ‹ › step a track into the deck
